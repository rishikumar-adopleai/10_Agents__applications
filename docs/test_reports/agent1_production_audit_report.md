## SECTION A: EXECUTIVE RESULT

### Verdict: **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS**

### Executive Summary
An exhaustive, code-level and data-level audit was conducted on Agent 1 (Buying-Signal Radar). The reported scan run (`4723cdc6-36e8-4dc3-ac22-b93fba567bbb`) previously generated an HTTP 200 OK and a Top-20 shortlist of accounts with total scores exceeding 0.95. However, deep-tier inspection revealed that **249 of the 249 reported buying signals were invalid, fabricated, misattributed, or stale**.

Specifically:
1. **HubSpot Engagement Signals (95 of 249)**: 100% fabricated mock fallback data generated by `HubSpotAdapter` when unauthorized or unconfigured.
2. **Regulatory Mandate Signals (152 of 249)**: 139 signals falsely attached payer-specific interoperability rules (CMS-0057-F and CMS-9115-F) to acute-care hospitals and clinics; 1 signal falsely attached a FinCEN AML banking mandate to East Jefferson General Hospital due to the geographical phrase "East Bank of Jefferson Parish".
3. **SEC EDGAR Signals (2 of 249)**: Misattributed public company CIKs (Mondelez International and Healthcare Realty Trust) to healthcare clinics (CHAS Health and Emergent Holdings) via substring matching, fabricated material weakness findings without reading 10-K Item 9A text, and were already stale (>90 days old).
4. **GDELT Growth Signals (0 detected)**: GDELT articles were discarded due to missing `source_provider` metadata and schema rejection by `EvidenceValidationService`.
5. **ATS Hiring Signals (0 detected)**: Greenhouse/Lever connectors could not resolve board tokens from generic company domains and lacked fallback coverage logging.
6. **ICP Evaluation**: Allowed 95 healthcare companies to achieve 0.90+ ICP fit against a Financial Services / KYC & Fraud profile because missing country defaulted to US, healthcare/banking terms shared an uncalibrated bucket, and operational fit defaulted to 0.75 without keyword matches.

### Remediation & Verification Summary
All identified code defects across configuration, adapters, validation, scoring, prioritization, idempotency, event contracts, downstream outcome tracking, SSRF defense, and CSV sanitization were corrected.

The updated codebase was verified using:

- **Full Backend Platform Regression Suite** (48 tests): 100% PASS (48 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped).

## SECTION G: REMAINING LIMITATIONS & RISKS

1. **Unconfigured HubSpot Connector (`NOT_LIVE_VERIFIED`)**:
   - `ENABLE_HUBSPOT=false` is currently set in `.env` with a placeholder access token (`mock-hubspot-token`).
   - The connector safely handles unconfigured states without crashing or fabricating data. However, until a real HubSpot Private App token with `crm.objects.companies.read` and `crm.objects.contacts.read` scopes is configured, HubSpot engagement signals will not be collected.
2. **Public ATS Board Token Resolution**:
   - Greenhouse and Lever adapters derive board tokens using domain subdomains (e.g., `company.com` -> `company`).
   - Companies using non-standard job board URLs (e.g., `boards.greenhouse.io/corp-careers`) require explicit metadata mapping or board token discovery.
3. **GDELT Indexing Latency**:
   - GDELT DOC 2.0 API is free and zero-cost, but has a 15-to-60 minute indexing delay. Breaking news within the last 15 minutes may not appear immediately.
4. **SEC EDGAR Politeness & Rate Limits**:
   - SEC EDGAR requires a compliant `User-Agent` header with contact email and limits traffic to 10 requests/second. The adapter enforces this compliance, but batch scans of thousands of public companies must be throttled accordingly.
